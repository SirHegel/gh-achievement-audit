# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of
experience, identity, background, or ability. Participation should make the project safer,
clearer, and more useful for contributors and maintainers.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal characteristics.
- Be precise, patient, and open to correction.
- Respect repository policies, maintainer decisions, and other contributors' time.
- Protect credentials, private repository details, and personal information.
- Disclose material AI assistance and other relevant conflicts as required by
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, impersonation, publishing another person's private information, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable. Spam, fabricated evidence, and activity created only to
manipulate contribution metrics are also outside this project's community standards.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project,
including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and related community channels.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/SirHegel/gh-achievement-audit/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `[Conduct]`. This shared private intake prevents reporters from
having to publish sensitive evidence; a conduct report is not treated as an assertion that
a software vulnerability exists. Do not include credentials or unrelated private data.
The maintainer will review reports confidentially, seek enough context for a fair decision,
and avoid public disclosure beyond what is necessary to keep the community safe.

Consequences will be proportional to impact and recurrence. They may include a private
correction, a formal warning, temporary limits on participation, or a permanent ban.
Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a violation.
